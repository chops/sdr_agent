The producer rechecks the current actor, source and exact binding under its approval locks. A successful approval would queue the draft for captured delivery; it would not send a real email. The next list reflects the committed source state.

R3/H3/T3 are symbolic example identifiers, not API-valid values or actual approvals.

Modeled inspection

Open the source detail through its authorized, audited read. The list did not load the draft body, research payload or model output. A withheld result does not become readable because an Inspect link was shown.

Modeled edit

A fresh edit creates R4 with H4/T4. D1 stays the same item and remains pending. An old R3 approval cannot carry forward. Compare-and-edit must be protected in the producer transaction; an unavailable adapter keeps this action blocked.

Modeled rejection

Submit the displayed revision and content hash with your reason. A committed rejection removes D1 from pending work; it does not suppress a contact or invent a new campaign rule.

Modeled stale command

R4/T4, or a new recipient pat@orbit.example.org, committed before your old command. Result: stale / revision or stale / recipient. No approval, capture or overwrite. Refresh and make a new decision on the displayed source.

Modeled investigation

Read F2 and its authorized run context. Only an active human admin can change a grant. Even after budget is available, unconfirmed cleanup or an unpromoted model tuple still blocks rerun. The agent never raises a budget or blindly repeats an unknown call.

Modeled acknowledgment

F2 becomes acknowledged with a new token and stays in the inbox. Acknowledgment is not resolution, cleanup confirmation or permission to spend.

Modeled resolution

A source-authorized resolution with your note removes that Failure after commit. Historical audit and run causes remain. If a separate Operation Failure F4 is live, it is still shown; the list does not collapse all conditions on one lead into “fixed.”

Other contract states

Read-only actor: an auditor may inspect permitted records, not approve or rerun. Forged commands fail source authorization and are audited.

Partial read: a failed Failure query cannot hide behind a successful Draft query. Authorized drafts may be shown, but the page warns that its view is incomplete.

Complete empty initial view: “No drafts or failures need your attention in this view.” “Nothing needs your attention” waits for complete MVP producer coverage, not merely these two sources.

More work: the first 50 items carry a continuation cursor; no unread candidate is silently skipped.
