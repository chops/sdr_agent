* Boundaries shown in the design (must survive into the contract)
- Search sources are shown as not set up; nothing searches or spends until
  the owner adds keys under a separate decision.
- One active target per company, with history; a second person only by a
  referral handover (C3's admission rule).
- People at an account are suggestions with a citation, not leads.
- Contact candidates (email, phone, LinkedIn URL) are one row per source,
  unconfirmed, never auto-promoted, never written to the CRM; the owner
  confirms each on Home.
- The owner's own method is an explicit placeholder: not guessed.
- Attended LinkedIn runs in the owner's browser, signed in by the owner;
  deterministic and human-paced; stops on CAPTCHA with no evasion; no model
  drives it; captures name and profile URL only. Private citation
  screenshots stay on the owner's machine (D-110).

* Neutral walk script
First explain only: "These are fictional examples. Nothing you click will
search, spend or contact anyone." Then ask, without a suggested answer:
- Targets: "What would you do on this screen?"
- Account: "What can you tell about this company from here?"
- People: "What would you do with these people?"
- Contact candidates: "What do you understand about these entries?"
- LinkedIn: "Walk me through how you'd use this."
Clarify unfamiliar wording if asked, but do not teach the intended answer.
Record first interpretations, actions, confusing labels and requests before
discussing internals. Confusion or rejection is useful evidence.
