* Neutral walk script
First explain only: "These are fictional examples. Nothing you click will
change data or send an email. Imagine these arrived while you were away."
Then ask, without a suggested answer:
- Normal: "What do you think needs you here? What would you do first?"
- Draft detail: "What would happen if you chose this action?"
- Changed: "What do you think happened? What would you do next?"
- Partial: "What can you tell from this screen, and what can't you tell?"
- Empty: "What does this screen tell you about your work?"
Clarify unfamiliar task wording if asked, but do not teach the desired
answer. Record first interpretation, actions, confusing labels and requests
before discussing internals. Rejection/confusion is useful evidence.
